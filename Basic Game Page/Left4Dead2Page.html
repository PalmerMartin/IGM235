Left 4 Dead 2

Description

Left 4 Dead 2 takes place one week after the events of the first game and three weeks after the first infection.
Instead of following the journey of Zoey, Francis, Louis and Bill from the first game, the player gets to control Coach, Rochelle, Nick or Ellis.
They arrived a few minutes too late at the evacuation point and now need to fight their way through the south-eastern part of infected America to the next official evacuation point.

Publishing Info

  • Developed by Valve Corporation
